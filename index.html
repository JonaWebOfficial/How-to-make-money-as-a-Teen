💡 Money Tips

Making money takes time. Start small and build useful skills.

🎯 Set a Goal

Decide what you are saving for and keep track of your progress.

📚 Learn Skills

Skills such as editing, coding and design can become useful over time.

⚠️ Avoid Scams

Be careful with anyone promising huge amounts of money for almost no work.

ℹ️ About Teen Money

Teen Money is a project designed to give teenagers ideas for safe and realistic ways to learn about earning and managing money.

Earnings shown on this website are examples, not guarantees. Rules about working and earning money can also vary depending on your age and country.
